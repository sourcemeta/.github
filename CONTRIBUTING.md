Developer Certificate of Origin
-------------------------------

Every Sourcemeta project enforces the [Developer Certificate of Origin
(DCO)](https://developercertificate.org), a statement introduced by the Linux
Foundation to ensure the contributor is allowed to make the contribution and
that the project may distribute it under its license. It requires you to
sign off your [Git](https://git-scm.com) commits, which you can do with the
[`--signoff`](https://git-scm.com/docs/git-commit#Documentation/git-commit.txt---signoff)
command-line flag.

Signing off a commit has two cumulative effects: you certify the Developer
Certificate of Origin and you accept the Contribution License below for the
corresponding contribution. The Contribution License is in addition to, and
independent of, the Developer Certificate of Origin and the project license,
and prevails over both as to the rights you grant to Sourcemeta.
